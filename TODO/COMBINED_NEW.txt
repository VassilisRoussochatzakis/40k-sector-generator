# COMBINED PROPOSED FEATURES FOR `sectorforge`

This document combines and deduplicates the suggested features from both proposal documents, presenting a unified vision for `sectorforge`. The guiding constraint across all proposals remains: every feature must support deterministic generation, offline data-driven configuration, rich political state, and reproducible exports. 

---

## 1. Historical Timeline and Sector Chronicle Generator
### What it is
A history pass that produces a deterministic, chronological backstory for the sector. It turns a static political snapshot into a setting with inherited grudges, old treaties, and past wars. It generates a dated list of events (or era-labelled events like `Founding`, `Schism`, `War`, `Rebellion`) that explain current claims and conflict states.
### How it works
Runs as a derived pipeline stage (`history`), seeded deterministically. It walks each world's claim list and emits events from a deterministic event grammar (authored in TOML). The generator derives the chronicle from current state (e.g., if a route is perilous, the history explains *when* and *how* it became so). 
### Surfacing & Data
- Configured via `history.toml` with event templates, preconditions, and eras.
- GUI: A "Timeline" tab where events cross-link to worlds and factions.
- CLI/Export: `history.md` exports per-system chronicles and sector-wide event digests.

## 2. Constraint-Directed Generation and Seed Search ("Wishes")
### What it is
Allows users to declare what they want in a sector (e.g., "exactly one forge world," "at least one warzone within two jumps of the capital") and have the tool find a seed that satisfies it automatically, rather than manual rerolling.
### How it works
Evaluates a small predicate DSL against finished sector models during a deterministic seed search. It checks candidates and stops at the first satisfying seed, or reports the closest near-miss. 
### Surfacing & Data
- Configured via `wishes.toml` (or a `constraints` file) with bounds, requirements, and logic.
- GUI: A "Wishes" or "Find a sector" panel with constraint dropdowns and progress bars.
- CLI: `sectorforge search <project> --wishes wishes.toml`

## 3. Dramatis Personae, Named NPCs, and Institutional Offices
### What it is
Generates a deterministic cast of named individuals tied to the political structure: planetary governors, sector lords, Rogue Trader captains, and cult magi. Separates the *Office* (a durable institutional role) from the *Personage* (the individual occupying it), allowing campaign state to replace characters without deleting the role.
### How it works
Runs as a `personae` stage seeded deterministically. Draws names, titles, and traits from user-supplied name pools. Generates one-line agendas based on the local political situation.
### Surfacing & Data
- Configured via `personae.toml` for name pools, title pools, traits, and office generation rules.
- GUI: Faction chips expand to show key office-holders.
- Export: A "Dramatis Personae" Markdown chapter.

## 4. Inter-Faction Diplomacy and Relationship Matrix
### What it is
An explicit deterministic model of how factions relate to each other (e.g., Allied, Neutral, Rival, At War). Replaces implicit assumptions with stated relationships, explaining *why* factions are fighting.
### How it works
A `relations` stage computes `FactionRelation` records for every pair based on faction kind, disposition, and TOML rules, allowing for asymmetrical attitudes (e.g., public trust vs. secret hostility). 
### Surfacing & Data
- GUI: A Diplomacy matrix view / relationship graph showing stances and causes.
- Export: "Factions at war" digest or diplomacy chapter.

## 5. Regional Warp Phenomena and Large-Scale Map Overlay
### What it is
Adds contiguous regions on the hex grid carrying warp conditions (e.g., Warp Storm, Calm Corridor) that modify world generation and routing inside their footprint.
### How it works
Computed before routes and worlds. Shapes are seeded deterministically and tagged with conditions. These modify candidate pool weights and route hazard tiers.
### Surfacing & Data
- GUI/PNG: Renders as a translucent tinted overlay under the hex grid.

## 6. Narrative Prose / Gazetteer Generator
### What it is
A deterministic template/grammar engine that turns the structured model into readable narrative text (e.g., Imperial-dispatch summaries, gazetteer entries). Not an LLM.
### How it works
A `prose` stage uses Backus-style grammar filled from the model, varying synonyms via deterministic seed.
### Surfacing & Data
- GUI: "Read as gazetteer" toggle rendering prose over structured tables.

## 7. Adventure Hooks, Quests, and Mission Seed Generator
### What it is
Turns the latent political tension into actionable, runnable adventure hooks or missions (e.g., Investigate, Escort, Sabotage) anchored to specific generated entities.
### How it works
Matches model conditions (e.g., contested worlds, perilous routes) to TOML templates, instantiating prompts with real factions, locations, complications, and stakes.
### Surfacing & Data
- GUI/Export: A "Hooks" or "Missions" tab and Markdown sections showing patrons, objectives, and hidden complications.

## 8. Sector Analytics, Balance Dashboard, and Interestingness Reports
### What it is
A computed report characterizing a sector's health, balance, and "interestingness" (faction dominance concentration, chokepoints, contested-world ratio, world-type distribution). 
### How it works
A read-only pass over the sector. Configurable thresholds can flag if a sector is "too safe" or "too chaotic."
### Surfacing & Data
- GUI: Dashboard tab with charts (faction power bars, connectivity callouts, scorecard).
- CLI: Fails CI builds if balance is broken.

## 9. Scenario Presets / Template Library
### What it is
Curated configuration bundles (e.g., *Embattled Frontier*, *Chaos Incursion*) that bias generation toward recognizable narrative shapes to ease new user onboarding.
### How it works
Presets are pure config overlays over the base schema.

## 10. Sector Diff & State-Comparison Tool
### What it is
Compares two sectors (or two states of the same sector) to report exactly what changed: lost sovereign slots, route hazard shifts, or differences between generator versions.
### How it works
A pure semantic comparison of two loaded sector models using stable IDs.
### Surfacing & Data
- CLI: Outputs `diff.md`.
- GUI: A split/overlay map where changed hexes pulse and a changelog is listed.

## 11. Self-Contained Interactive HTML Export
### What it is
A single, offline `.html` file containing the full sector map, UI, and data (inlined JSON + vanilla JS) to easily share explorable maps with players or GMs without needing the desktop app.

## 12. Trade, Resource Economy, and Strategic Value Layer
### What it is
A light, deterministic economy mapping world types to resource inputs/outputs (ore, food, recruits, arms). Gives concrete reasons for blockades and defines route trade volumes.
### How it works
Calculates surpluses/deficits and trade flows along routes, potentially feeding back into famine/rebellion stability logic.

## 13. External-Tool Export Adapters
### What it is
First-party modular adapters to export the sector directly into common downstream tools (e.g., Foundry VTT, Obsidian markdown vaults, Roll20).

## 14. Multi-Sector / Segmentum Composition (Atlas Mode)
### What it is
Composes several independently generated sectors into a larger "super-region" or segmentum map with cross-sector warp routes and regional summaries.
### How it works
A `stitch` stage bridges separate sector outputs based on proximity policies, preserving the original seeds.

## 15. Scripting / Plugin Hooks for Custom Generation Stages
### What it is
A sandboxed, embedded scripting engine (e.g., Rhai/Lua) allowing power users to register custom derivation stages (e.g., tagging worlds) while preserving the offline audit trail.

## 16. Campaign Fronts and Active Story Arcs
### What it is
Bundles raw conflict and intel into GM-ready "Fronts" (e.g., Genestealer Uprising, Naval Interdiction) with clocks, stages, and escalation paths. 
### How it works
Rule-based matching identifies high-pressure zones and groups them into actionable narrative threats, saving to `SectorSave`.

## 17. Planetary Sites and Point-of-Interest Generator
### What it is
Generates campaign-scale points of interest (e.g., Underhive safehouse, void elevator) on worlds to give missions concrete destinations.
### How it works
Derived from world type, surface regions, and local factions.

## 18. Threat Propagation and Crisis Forecasting
### What it is
A forecasting tool predicting where the sector might break next based on current states (e.g., Famine Risk, Blockade Escalation). Provides risk scores and recommended intervention points.

## 19. Player-Facing Redaction Profiles & Briefing Packs
### What it is
Allows generating targeted exports based on who is reading them. A "Player Profile" or "Imperial Navy Profile" automatically strips or falsifies hidden data and secret routes based on intel levels.

## 20. Mod Packs and Data Dependency Management
### What it is
A manifest system (`sectorforge-pack.toml`) for sharing and reusing custom data packages (name lists, faction sets, validation rules) without copying files manually.

## 21. Schema Versioning and Migration Tools
### What it is
Version numbering for serialized structures to safely upgrade existing campaign saves and `sector.json` files when the app updates, preventing loss of GM edits.

## 22. Themeable Map Rendering and Symbol Sets
### What it is
Separates map presentation from data. Users can choose themes (e.g., `print_mono`, `navis_tactical`, `imperial_archive`) for PNG/HTML exports to fit different visual needs (VTT vs. printed handouts).

## 23. Provenance, Explainability, and "Why?" Inspector
### What it is
A debug/explain tool that tells the user exactly *why* a specific generated event happened (e.g., why a faction is dominant, why a route is hazardous), displaying the exact candidate weights and rules that triggered it.

## 24. Validation Rule Authoring and Custom Invariants
### What it is
Allows project authors to define bespoke logical constraints in TOML (e.g., "Paradise worlds cannot have Chaos presence") that the application enforces during generation.

## 25. In-Application Changelog and Campaign Session Log
### What it is
A human-readable log stored in the `SectorSave` connecting generated state to actual table history, tracking manual state changes made by the GM as the campaign progresses.
